Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

1. What we collect

  • Account information: your name, email address and password. Your password is stored only in a securely hashed form, never in plain text.
  • Order information: shipping addresses, phone numbers, the items you buy, and your order history.
  • Saved addresses and wishlist items, if you choose to save them.
  • Reviews you write on products you have bought.
  • Payment information: card payments are handled by our payment partner, Razorpay. We do not see or store your full card number.

2. How we use it

To process and deliver your orders, manage your account, take payment, and send you emails about your orders (confirmations, shipping updates and cancellations). We do not sell your personal data to anyone.

The scrolling "recent purchases" bar on our site shows the product bought and the city it is being delivered to. It never shows names, addresses or any other details.

3. Who we share it with

  • Razorpay, our payment partner, receives the order amount and your phone number to complete online payments.
  • Our email provider delivers the order emails we send to you.
  • When you type a PIN code at checkout, that PIN code is sent to India Post's public PIN code directory so we can suggest your city and state. No other details are sent.
  • Delivery partners receive your name, address and phone number so they can deliver your order.

We may also share information when the law requires it.

4. Logging in and storage in your browser

When you sign in, a login token is kept in your browser so you stay signed in while you browse. It is needed for the site to work and is not used for advertising or for tracking you across other sites.

5. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data at any time by writing to us at admin@shopsphere.com. You can also delete your saved addresses yourself from your account.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your account and order records for as long as your account is active, and for as long as the law requires us to keep them (for example, tax and accounting records).

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "last updated" date above.

8. Contact

Questions about your data? Write to us at admin@shopsphere.com.
